Questions

Good to know before you join

How much does a course cost?

Each course has its own one-time fee. On 12 November 2026 the catalogue listed 48 courses priced from ₦2,500 to ₦9,998. Students do not pay a monthly subscription.

How do I pay?

Through Paystack, by card, bank transfer or USSD. You can start learning as soon as your payment goes through.

Is the certificate included in the course fee?

No. After you finish every module and pass the exam, you can get the certificate for a separate ₦10,000 fee. It is optional.

How does the exam work?

It unlocks when you complete every module. There are 20 multiple-choice questions, the pass mark is 60% and you get 2 attempts. If you use both, contact the academy to have your exam reset.

How can someone check that my certificate is real?

Every certificate has a unique ID. Anyone can enter it on the academy's public verification page at creators-hub-academy.vercel.app/verify.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, if you ask within 7 days of buying and have completed 20% or less of the course. Requests are reviewed case by case. Once a certificate has been issued or the course is recorded as completed, the fee is not refundable.

How do teachers get paid?

Students pay through Paystack and you keep 90% of each enrollment. You follow your sales in the teacher dashboard, add your bank details and request a payout, which the academy admin then processes.

What does it cost to teach?

Teachers need a subscription: ₦12,500 monthly, ₦33,750 quarterly or ₦135,000 yearly. Teacher applications are approved by the academy admin.

What do I get for referring friends?

Points: 100 when a friend signs up with your link, 250 when they enrol in a course and 500 when they complete one. Rewards are points on your account, not cash.

I am a freelancer. Which account do I need?

A normal learner account. There is no separate freelancer account. When you are ready to sell your own course, apply to become a teacher.
